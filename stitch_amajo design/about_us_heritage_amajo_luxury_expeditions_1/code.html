Genesis & Provenance

Born Under the Volcanic Canopy, Raised on Savannah Whispers

Before AMAJO existed on international maps, its founders walked the damp trails of Volcanoes National Park and the cracked sunbeds of the Great Rift Valley. They carried notebooks, tracking lines passed down through four generations of indigenous trackers, and an unshakeable reverence for the Great Apes and migrating megafauna.

For decades, international operators sold East Africa through distant desks in London and Geneva, repatriating revenues far from the community forest borders where trackers put their lives on the line. We rebelled against that extraction model.

AMAJO unified generational wildlife trackers with bespoke bush architects to prove that ultra-luxury hospitality and uncompromising sovereign conservation are not opposing forces—they are the only sustainable path forward.
