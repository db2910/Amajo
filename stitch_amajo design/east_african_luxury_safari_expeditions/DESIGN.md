## Brand & Style

This design system channels the refined elegance of high-end conservation hospitality across East Africa—capturing the untamed landscapes of Rwanda's volcanic mists, Uganda's rainforests, and Kenya's golden savannas. The aesthetic balances high-editorial storytelling akin to vintage natural history publications with the frictionless, high-utility booking mechanics of premier contemporary travel applications.

### Core Visual Tenets
- **Editorial Luxury:** Generous whitespace, deliberate letter-spacing, and evocative serif headlines create an atmosphere of unhurried elegance and distinguished taste.
- **Warm Earth & Tactility:** Pure digital white and cold grays are omitted in favor of sun-bleached linen, warm ivory, and fertile earth tones that echo the tactile qualities of handmade field journals and bespoke safari pavilions.
- **Modern Intimacy:** User interfaces favor warmth and approachability over aloof corporate minimalism, utilizing gentle geometry and thoughtful micro-transitions to make bespoke adventure planning effortless and inspiring.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from the warm earth, sunset horizons, and rich textures of East Africa.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#D95B26`):** Burnt Orange. Employs the vibrant, luminous energy of equatorial dawns and the emblematic elephant crest. Reserved for focal call-to-actions, booking triggers, active navigational states, and key badges.
- **Secondary (`#2C140E`):** Deep Dark Cocoa. Rooted in volcanic fertile soil and rich African timber. Acts as the primary anchor for luxury serif headlines, heavy editorial contrasts, structural dark surfaces, and prominent navigation headers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359`):** Savannah Gold. Used for subtle luxury accents, star ratings, expedition highlights, curated tags, and premium member signifiers.
- **Neutral (`#FBF8F3`):** Warm Ivory. Replaces stark optical white across page canvas backgrounds, card surfaces, and elevated dialogs, providing a rich, paper-like warmth that softens screen fatigue.

### Supporting Earth Accents
- **Terracotta & Rust (`#BF4821` / `#942E13`):** Semantic hover, pressed, and focus states for primary components.
- **Sage Canopy (`#4A5D4E`):** Used sparingly for conservation indicators, eco-accreditations, and natural landscape status tags.
- **Muted Sand (`#EFE8DE`):** Structural borders, subtle dividers, and inactive state fills.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a clear tension between heritage storytelling and functional modern clarity.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the historical pedigree and romance of high-end travel journalism. Used for hero phrases, destination names, lodge profiles, and editorial narratives. It should never be set in all caps, relying instead on its natural title-case grace and high stroke contrast.
- **Interface & Content (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides neutral, highly legible utility for itinerary schedules, pricing matrices, booking modals, and form elements. 
- **Eyebrows & Metadata:** Labels such as region tags, duration markers, and tracking tags are set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, semi-bold or bold, with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) and uppercase styling to anchor complex travel itineraries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model embraces an unhurried, editorial pacing inspired by architectural pavilions and coffee-table publications.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid within a constrained max-width of `1360px`. Gutters are standard `2rem` (32px), with outer page padding expanding up to `5rem` (80px) on wide displays to ensure immersion without visual sprawl.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Itinerary timelines and dual-column lodge details transition seamlessly into stacked or staggered compositions.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal margins. Bottom-sheet booking bars and horizontally scrolling lodge media carousels maximize vertical screen real estate.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Intra-Component Spacing (`0.5rem` to `1rem`):** Tight, systematic relationships between icons, badges, and their adjacent textual descriptors.
- **Section Spacing (`4rem` to `8rem`):** Substantial negative space between content sections creates a serene browsing cadence that mirrors luxury hospitality.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft, sun-dappled tactile shadows and subtle tonal layering rather than cold synthetic blurs or sharp dropped shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Architecture
- **Tinted Ambient Occlusion:** All shadows incorporate a soft warm brown/cocoa undertone (`rgba(44, 20, 14, ...)`) instead of neutral pitch-black, mirroring natural outdoor lighting conditions.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Inputs):** `0px 2px 8px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.02)`. Grounded, discrete separation from Warm Ivory canvas.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Itinerary Cards, Dropdowns):** `0px 10px 24px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.07), 0px 4px 8px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.03)`. Gives the impression of a card rising gently from a table.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Sticky Booking Bars, Floating Sheets):** `0px 20px 40px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.06)`. Unmistakable focal hierarchy with full backdrop dimming using Cocoa at `50%` opacity (`rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.5)`).

### Outlines & Borders
- Key content boundaries use delicate hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(44, 20, 14, 0.08)`) to preserve crisp alignment in high-density booking tools without adding visual weight.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `2`, the shape language delivers a balanced, inviting architectural touch: crisp enough to honor structural precision and editorial sophistication, yet soft enough to feel organic and hospitable.

### Token Application
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers tactile precision and clarity.
- **Cards, Modals, & Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px). Creates soft containment for photography-rich safari packages, regional maps, and guest reviews.
- **Feature Modules & Image Frames (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px). Best suited for flagship hero galleries and prominent story vignettes.
- **Tags & Floating Actions (`rounded-full`):** Pill-shaped contours for status indicators (e.g., "Gorilla Permit Included", "Migratory Season") and circular media controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Burnt Orange (`#D95B26`) background, Warm Ivory (`#FBF8F3`) text, rounded to `0.5rem` (8px). Medium font weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), subtle inner warmth. Hover state shifts to Terracotta (`#BF4821`) with Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary (Editorial Dark):** Deep Dark Cocoa (`#2C140E`) fill with Warm Ivory text for high-contrast, premium actions (e.g., "Request Bespoke Itinerary").
- **Tertiary (Ghost / Outline):** Transparent background with `1.5px` border in Deep Dark Cocoa or Burnt Orange, accompanied by matching typography. Hover introduces an ivory wash (`rgba(217, 91, 38, 0.06)`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Neutral:** Warm Ivory or white surface, hairline sand border (`#EFE8DE`), Deep Dark Cocoa text.
- **State Active:** Solid Deep Dark Cocoa or Burnt Orange background with contrasting light text and no visible border.
- Used extensively in the itinerary explorer for destination tagging (e.g., "Serengeti", "Volcanoes NP", "Bwindi").

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background set to pure `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#F5EFEB` with a `1px` subtle outline in Sand (`#E0D7CB`). Text entered is rendered in Deep Dark Cocoa.
- Focus state brings an explicit `1.5px` Burnt Orange ring and a gentle ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 91, 38, 0.15)`).
- Date and guest pickers feature prominent, legible numeric display paired with understated labels.

### Cards (Tours & Lodges)
- Built on Warm Ivory container bases with `1rem` corner rounding and Level 1 elevation.
- Photography features a mandatory subtle inset ratio (`4:3` or `16:10`) with slight zoom-on-hover micro-interactions (`transform: scale(1.02)` over 400ms cubic-bezier).
- Visual hierarchy prioritizes: Editorial Tag (Savannah Gold/Sage) → Headline (Playfair Display) → Itinerary Key Meta (Days, Pace, Regions) → Price and Reservation CTA.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls feature a dual concentric ring: external Deep Cocoa frame with an inner active Burnt Orange dot.
- Checkboxes utilize a soft square (`0.25rem` radius) with Burnt Orange checkfills and crisp ivory glyphs.

### Additional Domain-Specific Components
- **Itinerary Timeline Node:** Vertical connecting rail in Savannah Gold/Muted Sand with Cocoa timeline markers denoting morning game drives, private bush flights, and luxury lodge dinners.
- **Conservation Badge:** Pill-shaped micro-component in Sage Earth (`#4A5D4E`) signaling habitat sustainability contributions, community royalties, and ranger support metrics.
