Curated Narrative

Two Mythic Landscapes, One Sublime Passage

Few journeys anywhere on Earth contrast raw primordial power with vast cinematic openness like East Africa’s dual sanctuaries. Begin within Rwanda’s mystical Virunga volcanoes, stepping into humid ancient bamboo to sit eye-to-eye with an endangered mountain gorilla family in quiet contemplation. Then, board your private charter wing as the Great Rift Valley unfolds beneath you, touching down directly onto the private conservancies of the Maasai Mara for uncrowded game drives, golden lion prides, and the thunderous pulse of the migration.

Guest Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

How physically demanding is the gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park?

add
Trekking times range from 45 minutes to 3.5 hours depending on where the habituated family is feeding along the volcanic slopes. The terrain can be muddy and uneven, but AMAJO assigns private porters to carry your daypack and assist with footing. Treks are matched carefully to your comfort level during morning park briefings.

What luggage specifications are required for private bush charters?

add
For inner-safari flights aboard Cessna Grand Caravans, soft-sided duffel bags without rigid frames are requested (maximum 15 kg / 33 lbs per person). Complimentary AMAJO luxury leather duffels can be provided upon arrival, and excess city luggage is securely stored in Nairobi or Kigali.

When is the best time to witness the Great Migration river crossings?

add
The massive herds of over 1.5 million wildebeest and zebras typically traverse the Mara River between late July and late October. However, because our itineraries feature private conservancies, magnificent resident predator action and big cats are exceptional year-round.
