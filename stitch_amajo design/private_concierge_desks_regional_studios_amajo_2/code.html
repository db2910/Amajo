Frequently Asked Consultation Questions

Key details regarding gorilla trekking permits, private aircraft operations, and dietary orchestration in remote East African concessions.

Rwanda allocates only 96 permits daily across its habituated gorilla families, and Uganda has similar daily caps. For peak travel months (June through October and late December), permits often book out 6 to 12 months in advance. Because AMAJO maintains a registered in-country license with both the Rwanda Development Board (RDB) and Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA), we can hold official allocations during your initial consultation phase.
Yes. Our Nairobi and Kigali flight desks coordinate directly with Civil Aviation Authorities in Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania. We arrange private hangar parking, ramp transfers, fast-track diplomatic or private aircraft customs, and direct inter-park bush charter legs to avoid commercial transit bottlenecks.
Wildlife permits (such as Gorilla Habituation at $1,500 USD per person in Rwanda or $700/$1,500 in Uganda) require 100% immediate prepayment to the statutory bodies upon reservation. Lodge accommodations and private air charters generally require a 25% to 30% deposit upon itinerary confirmation, with balance due 60 to 90 days prior to arrival.
Our senior concierge briefs executive camp chefs in advance of your touchdown. Whether requiring strict kosher-style preparation, organic gluten-free provisions, or specific allergen protocols, fresh supplies are chartered in. For gorilla treks, we arrange specialized sedan-chair porter teams ('Heli-treks' and 'palanquin' carriers) so every guest can encounter mountain gorillas comfortably regardless of mobility limits.
